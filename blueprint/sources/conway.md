# Conway 1970

J.H. Conway, *An Enumeration of Knots and Links, and Some of Their Algebraic
Properties*, in J. Leech (ed.), *Computational Problems in Abstract Algebra*,
Pergamon, Oxford, 1970, pp. 329–358.

This is the origin of rational-tangle notation and of the classification
theorem. Kauffman–Lambropoulou cite it as [8] and attribute Theorem 1 to
Conway (1970). It is **cited, not adopted**: the working proof is the 2004
paper, and this repository does not contain the 1970 PDF.

## What is cited

- The name and notation of rational tangles, integer tangles, and the
  continued-fraction form of a rational tangle.
- The statement that two rational tangles are isotopic if and only if they
  have the same fraction (Kauffman–Lambropoulou, Theorem 1).
- The broader programme of enumerating knots and links by inserting rational
  tangles into planar graphs. That census is out of scope for the first
  milestone; see the [coverage page](../coverage/README.md).

## What is not used as a proof text

Conway's 1970 definition of the fraction via continued fractions, and any
enumeration tables or algebraic identities that are not restated in
Kauffman–Lambropoulou §§1–5, are not taken as formalization targets. Where the
2004 paper records a Conway convention that it then *reverses* (crossing
signs, p. 6), the 2004 convention is the one this project follows.
