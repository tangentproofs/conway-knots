# Unrestricted slides on negative two-unit diagrams

On `[-1]+[-1]` and `[-1]*[-1]`, every coloring transports across the
Figure 5 slide with no `DiagonalSum` hypothesis: the target interior
arcs are forced by the boundary match, and every remaining equation
reduces to a source-validity identity closed by linear arithmetic. The
negative units are mirror-switches of the positive ones, so the arc
wirings differ from the `[+1]` case; both slides are evaluated directly.
Together with the positive twin, `DiagonalSum` is sufficient but not
necessary on all same-sign two-unit diagrams — the hypothesis is
load-bearing only on larger diagrams. Not unrestricted slides in
general. Proof part of the parent topic; see that page for the
mathematical context.
