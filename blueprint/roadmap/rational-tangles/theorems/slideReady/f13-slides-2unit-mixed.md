# Unrestricted slides on mixed-sign two-unit diagrams

On all four ordered mixed-sign pairs (`[+1]+[-1]`, `[-1]+[+1]`,
`[+1]*[-1]`, `[-1]*[+1]`), every coloring transports across the
Figure 5 slide with no `DiagonalSum` hypothesis: the target interior
arcs are forced by the boundary match, and every remaining equation
reduces to a source-validity identity closed by linear arithmetic. The
canceling structure (fraction `0`) does not obstruct transport, which
uses only the linear coloring rules. Together with the same-sign twins,
unconditional two-unit slides are now complete in all eight ordered
sign cases. Not unrestricted slides in general. Proof part of the
parent topic; see that page for the mathematical context.
