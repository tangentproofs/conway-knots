# Invert-mul of two-block vertical products

Invert-mul of the two-block vertical product of canceling diagrams
(`coloring_invert_mul_two_rightBottom` when $n \neq 0$, dual to
invert-add) carries $0$ on both orders. Not a `ColoringIsotopy`
constructor: invert-mul switches crossings. Proof part of the parent
topic; see that page for the mathematical context.
