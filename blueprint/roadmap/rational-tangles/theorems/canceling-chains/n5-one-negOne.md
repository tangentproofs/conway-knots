# Invert-add of $[1]+[-1]$ spelled out

The two-unit canceling pairs with literal `[1]`/`[-1]` summands: every
non-monochrome coloring has fraction $0$, and invert-add of each order
carries $\infty$. Specializations of the `canceling_units` pair to
`crossingTangle` signs. Proof part of the parent topic; see that page
for the mathematical context.
