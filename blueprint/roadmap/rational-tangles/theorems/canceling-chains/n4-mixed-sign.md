# Mixed-sign canceling pieces

Integer pieces with opposite signs summing to $0$
(`neg_integer_add`): every non-monochrome coloring has fraction $0$,
and invert-add of the mixed pair carries $\infty$. Sign-flipped
variants on both sides. Proof part of the parent topic; see that page
for the mathematical context.
