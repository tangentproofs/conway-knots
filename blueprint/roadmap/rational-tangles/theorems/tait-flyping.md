# Tait flyping conjecture

Two alternating knots are isotopic if and only if any two corresponding
diagrams on $S^2$ are related by a finite sequence of flypes.

This is Tait's 1877 working assumption, proved by Menasco and Thistlethwaite
in 1993. Kauffman–Lambropoulou assume the corresponding statement for vertex
closures of alternating 2-tangles (citing Thistlethwaite) and derive from it
the flyping statement for alternating *rational* tangles (Proposition 4).
The result is background, not a theorem proved in the adopted paper.

This article is retained as exposition of the unused §4 flyping argument.
It is not a dependency of Theorem 1: the classification is proved via the
coloring fraction of §5. Tait is not an axiom on the critical path.
