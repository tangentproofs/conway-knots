# Every rational tangle has a standard form

Every rational tangle can be brought via isotopy to standard form.

Starting from twist form, horizontal and vertical rational flypes (and the
commutativity supplied by the flipping lemma) move all horizontal twists to
the right and all vertical twists to the bottom.
