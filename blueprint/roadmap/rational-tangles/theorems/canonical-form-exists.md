# Every rational tangle isotopes to canonical form

Every rational tangle can be isotoped to canonical form: an alternating
continued-fraction form of odd length.

If a continued-fraction vector is not termwise of one sign, a transfer move
(Figure 14) together with flypes and flips removes a mixed-sign adjacent pair
and reduces the sum of absolute values of the entries, so induction produces
an alternating tangle. Length is then made odd by splitting the last term as
in Definition 6.
