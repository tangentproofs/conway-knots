# Invert-add with a left summand $[\infty]$

Fresh dummy colorings of $([\infty]+T)^{i}$ and of
$T^{i}*[\infty]^{i}$ on an arbitrary diagram $T$, agreeing at fraction
$0$. On a `slideReady` twist the same dummy colorings carry the formula
value $([\infty]+F)^{-1}$ (definitionally $0$ for every $F$). Proof part
of the parent topic; see that page for the mathematical context.
