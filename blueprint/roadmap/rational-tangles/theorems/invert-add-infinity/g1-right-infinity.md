# Invert-add with a right summand $[\infty]$

Fresh dummy colorings of $(T+[\infty])^{i}$ and of
$[\infty]^{i}*T^{i}$ on an arbitrary diagram $T$, agreeing at fraction
$0$. On a `slideReady` twist the same dummy colorings carry the formula
value $(F+[\infty])^{-1}$ (definitionally $0$ for every $F$). Proof part
of the parent topic; see that page for the mathematical context.
