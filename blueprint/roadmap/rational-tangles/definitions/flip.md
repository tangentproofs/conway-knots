# Flip

A **flip** is a rotation in space of a 2-tangle by $180^\circ$.

- $T^{h\mathrm{flip}}$ is the **horizontal flip**: rotation around a
  horizontal axis in the plane of $T$.
- $T^{v\mathrm{flip}}$ is the **vertical flip**: rotation around a vertical
  axis in the plane of $T$.

In general a flip switches endpoints and need not be an isotopy of 2-tangles.
For rational tangles it is, by the flipping lemma. A flype on a 2-subtangle
$t$ may be written
$$
[\pm 1]+t \;\sim\; t^{h\mathrm{flip}}+[\pm 1]
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
[\pm 1]\ast t \;\sim\; t^{v\mathrm{flip}}\ast[\pm 1].
$$
