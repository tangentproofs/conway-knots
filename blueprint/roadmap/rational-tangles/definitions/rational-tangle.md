# Rational tangle

A **rational tangle in twist form** is created by consecutive additions and
multiplications by the tangles $[\pm 1]$, starting from $[0]$ or $[\infty]$
(Definition 1). Equivalently (Note 1), it is a proper embedding of two
unoriented arcs $\alpha_1,\alpha_2$ in a 3-ball $B^3$ with four endpoints on
the boundary for which there exists a homeomorphism of pairs
$$
h:(B^3,\alpha_1,\alpha_2)\longrightarrow(D^2\times I,\{x,y\}\times I)
$$
to a trivial tangle.

Inductively, a twist-form rational tangle is obtained from a previously
created rational tangle by adding integer tangles and multiplying by vertical
tangles. Addition or multiplication of two non-integer rational tangles need
not remain rational.

This project takes Definition 1 as the working definition of a rational
tangle, with the homeomorphism-of-pairs description as the intended
topological meaning.
