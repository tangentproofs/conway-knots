# Tangle foundations

Definitions for the combinatorial theory of rational 2-tangles: Reidemeister
moves, 2-tangles and isotopy, integer and rational tangles, flypes and
flips, standard, continued-fraction, and canonical forms, the arithmetic
continued fraction, and the fraction $F(T)$ as the arithmetical evaluation
of the twist form.
