# Reidemeister moves

A **Reidemeister move** is one of the three local diagram changes R1, R2, R3
on a tangle (or knot) diagram, performed in the interior of the projection
disc and leaving the four endpoints of a 2-tangle fixed.

- **R1.** A kink may be added to or removed from a single strand (a twist of
  writhe $\pm 1$).
- **R2.** Two strands with two opposite crossings may be pulled apart, or two
  uncrossed strands may be overlapped to create two opposite crossings.
- **R3.** A strand may be slid over (or under) a crossing of two other
  strands.

Kauffman–Lambropoulou use these moves without listing the three pictures:
isotopy of 2-tangle diagrams is equivalent to a finite sequence of
Reidemeister moves in the interior of the disc (Introduction, arXiv PDF
p. 2, citing Reidemeister [31]). Section 5 uses the same moves as the
generators of diagram isotopy for coloring. This article spells out R1–R3
so that those uses are formalizable leaves rather than an imported axiom.

Planar isotopy of the diagram (no change of crossing data) is included with
the Reidemeister generators.
