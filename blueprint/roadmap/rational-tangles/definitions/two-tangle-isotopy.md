# 2-tangle and isotopy

A **2-tangle** is an embedding in a 3-ball $B^3$ of two arcs and a finite
number of circles, with the four endpoints of the arcs lying on $\partial B^3$
in the standard NW/NE/SW/SE positions. (Throughout, “tangle” means a tangle
diagram, as in the paper.)

Two rational tangles $T,S$ in $B^3$ are **isotopic**, written $T\sim S$, if
there is an orientation-preserving self-homeomorphism
$h:(B^3,T)\to(B^3,S)$ that is the identity on the boundary. Equivalently,
any two diagrams of $T$ and $S$ have identical endpoint configurations on the
projection disc and differ by a finite sequence of Reidemeister moves in the
interior of the disc.

The class of 2-tangles is closed under addition $T+S$, multiplication
$T\ast S$, mirror image $-T$, rotation $T^r$ (counterclockwise by
$90^\circ$), and inversion $T^i:=-T^r$. Numerator and denominator closures
$N(T)$ and $D(T)$ produce knots or links; they are used later to form vertex
closures, but are not themselves first-milestone targets.
