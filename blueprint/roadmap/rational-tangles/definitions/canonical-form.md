# Canonical form of a rational tangle

A rational tangle $T=[[\beta_1],[\beta_2],\ldots,[\beta_m]]$ in continued
fraction form is in **canonical form** if $T$ is alternating (all $\beta_i$
positive, or all negative) and $m$ is odd (Definition 6). If $T$ is
alternating and $m$ is even, the last term may be split by the identity
$[\beta_m]=[\mathrm{sign}(\beta_m)\cdot(|\beta_m|-1)]+[\mathrm{sign}(\beta_m)\cdot 1]$
to make the length odd.

$T$ is called positive or negative according to the sign of its terms.
