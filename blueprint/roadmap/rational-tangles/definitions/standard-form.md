# Standard form

A rational tangle is in **standard form** if it is created by consecutive
additions of $[\pm 1]$ only on the right (or only on the left) and
multiplications by $[\pm 1]$ only at the bottom (or only at the top), starting
from $[0]$ or $[\infty]$ (Definition 4).

Inductively, from a previously created rational tangle $T$, one either adds an
integer tangle on the right, $T\mapsto T+[\pm k]$, or multiplies by a vertical
tangle at the bottom, $T\mapsto T\ast[n]^{-1}$. The associated integer vector
$(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$ records the successive blocks of twists; $n$ may be taken
odd.
