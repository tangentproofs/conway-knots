# Negative invert-add ladder: factored rungs twelve

Rung forty-eight and above of the negative unit ladder, each split into
crossings preliminaries, a fraction-value preliminary, universal, witness,
witness-rules, existence-head, witness-value, and existence nodes (the flat universal would otherwise
exceed the span limit, and from rung twenty-nine the universal linarith tail is
factored out the same way; from rung thirty-six the existence tail is factored
likewise, and from rung thirty-eight the existence head — the crossing-list
`decide` plus the tangle-to-matrix `rfl` bridge — is factored likewise).
Opened as the sequel of the
[factored-rungs-eleven sub-chapter](../factored-rungs-eleven/README.md), which
holds 17 articles (README plus 16 article nodes, `a68`–`a69g`: an octet
cannot fit there, since 17 + 8 = 25 > 24),
so new rungs go here; the mathematics is
unchanged.
