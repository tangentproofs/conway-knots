# Unrestricted five-unit invert-add value

Every non-monochrome coloring of `([+1]+[+1]+[+1]+[+1]+[+1])ⁱ` has
fraction `1/5` (universal), with a direct witness for the existence:
the five switched rules force numerator `d` and denominator `5d` for
nonzero `d`, with no `DiagonalSum` or port inputs. Rung three of the
ladder; module build cost rises only slightly over the four-unit rung,
confirming the near-linear cost trend. Proof part of the parent topic;
see that page for the mathematical context.
