# Color matrix

Let $T$ be a colored 2-tangle. Write $\mathrm{NW}(T)$, $\mathrm{NE}(T)$,
$\mathrm{SW}(T)$ and $\mathrm{SE}(T)$ for the colors on the external strands
at the northwest, northeast, southwest and southeast positions. The **color
matrix** of $T$ is
$$
M(T)=\begin{pmatrix}\mathrm{NW}(T)&\mathrm{NE}(T)\\\mathrm{SW}(T)&\mathrm{SE}(T)\end{pmatrix}.
$$
