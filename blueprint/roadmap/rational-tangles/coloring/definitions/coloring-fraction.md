# Coloring fraction

Let $T$ be an integrally colored 2-tangle with color matrix
$M=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$. The **coloring fraction** is the
extended rational
$$
f(T):=\frac{b-a}{b-d},
$$
with the convention $f(T)=\infty=1/0$ when $b=d$ and $b\neq a$. (If $T$ is
colored but not rational, the same formula is used, but the value may depend
on the choice of coloring.)
