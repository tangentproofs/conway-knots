    Negative invert-add ladder: factored rungs five

    Rungs thirty-two and above of the negative unit ladder, each split into crossings preliminaries, a fraction-value preliminary, universal, witness, witness-rules, and existence nodes (the flat universal would otherwise exceed the span limit, and from rung twenty-nine the universal linarith tail is factored out the same way). Opened as the sequel of the factored-rungs-four sub-chapter, which is at the 24-article limit (a sextet cannot fit there: 19 + 6 = 25 > 24), so new rungs go here; the mathematics is unchanged.
